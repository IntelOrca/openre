Barlow-Regular.ttf / Barlow-Bold.ttf
=====================================

Barlow is a grotesque typeface by Jeremy Tribby, released under the SIL Open
Font License, Version 1.1.

    https://barlowfont.com
    https://openfontlicense.org / https://scripts.sil.org/OFL

The full OFL-1.1 licence text is available from the SIL OFL page above.
In brief:

* You may use, modify and redistribute the fonts freely.
* You may not sell the fonts by themselves, but embedding them in software
  (this project) is allowed.
* If you redistribute the modified fonts, they must remain under the OFL.
* The Reserved Font Name "Barlow" may not be used for derivative works.

This project bundles the unmodified Barlow Typeface files solely to provide a
configurable TTF backing font for the `[options] "font" = Modern` text mode.
